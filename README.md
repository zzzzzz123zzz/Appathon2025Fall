## 🪶 Overview
Write daily notes about your health, moods, or symptoms.  
The assistant gently asks follow‑ups and builds a weekly “At‑a‑Glance” card you can share with family or providers.

---

## ✨ Features
- Free‑text journaling and AI conversation
- Smart weekly summaries
- Personalization through a short onboarding quiz
- Lightweight reminders that keep you engaged
